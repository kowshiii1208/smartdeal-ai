🎓 Placement Interview & Viva Presentation Defense

Q1: Why exclude current_price from the regression feature set?

Answer: Including current_price would cause catastrophic target leakage. The objective is to determine what an item ought to cost based on intrinsic specifications and brand equity. If current_price were given to the regressor, it would memorize the identity mapping (y = x), showing trivial 0 loss while failing completely to evaluate whether the listing is a good deal.

Q2: Why did Linear Regression beat Random Forest and Gradient Boosting?

Answer: Fair reference pricing is fundamentally an economically proportional scalar problem directly tied to MSRP (Price ≈ (1 - δ) * MSRP + β * Rating). Linear models capture this continuous slope seamlessly (R² = 0.9894). In contrast, decision tree ensembles split continuous dimensions into step-wise constant buckets, leading to step boundary errors on continuous pricing scales with N=96 training points.

Q3: How does your deal scoring formula penalize weak evidence?

Answer: Products with fewer than 100 reviews are weighted with W_confidence = 0.75, which mathematically regresses the raw score back toward the fair equilibrium anchor of 50. This prevents unverified sellers or new listings with inflated 5-star ratings from triggering false "Great Deal" alerts.
